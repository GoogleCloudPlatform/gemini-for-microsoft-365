# OUTLOOK-01: Outlook Smart Reply & Calendar Assistant Specification

## 1. Overview
Provides contextual email thread summarization, smart grounded replies, and calendar briefing prep within Microsoft Outlook (Mail & Calendar).

## 2. Capabilities
- **Email Thread Summarization**: Summarizes complex multi-party conversations into key decisions and action items.
- **Grounded Smart Reply Drafter**: Extracts sender/recipient context and drafts professional replies matching corporate tone.
- **Meeting Briefing & Agenda Prep**: Extracts meeting attendee profiles and creates briefing agendas.
